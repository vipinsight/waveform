Text, how you like it

Optional AI Polish can tidy text locally or use a hosted model through OpenRouter.

Your words stay yours.

Audio is transcribed on your Mac. Waveform does not upload your voice. Your speech model runs locally after setup.

Optional AI Polish can also run on your Mac. If you choose OpenRouter, Waveform sends the text you ask it to polish to your selected hosted model; it never sends audio.

Model downloads and update checks use the network. You can turn off update checks in settings.

Pick a voice that fits your Mac.

Choose from Whisper, Parakeet, and Qwen3-ASR speech models. Compare model sizes, then download the one that suits your hardware and the way you speak.
