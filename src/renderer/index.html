      Waveform

      Type with your voice

      Waveform turns speech into text, anywhere in macOS, without sending it anywhere.

      1. 1 Hold a key One key you pick, held down in whatever app you are already in.
      2. 2 Say it Talk normally. A model on this Mac listens and writes it down.
      3. 3 Let go The text appears where your cursor already was.

      Your voice is transcribed on this Mac and never uploaded.
