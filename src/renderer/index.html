      Choose the key you hold to speak

      Hold it anywhere in macOS and talk; let go and the words land at your cursor. It has to be a key that types nothing on its own.

      Fn also switches input source by default. Set System Settings → Keyboard → Press 🌐 to to Do Nothing so a tap does not do both.

      Let macOS in on it

      Three permissions, each asked for once. macOS grants them to you, not to us, and revoking them is the same three switches.
