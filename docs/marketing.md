# Waveform marketing brief

## Positioning

Waveform is free, open-source voice dictation for Apple Silicon Macs. Hold a
shortcut, speak, release: your words appear in the app already under your
cursor. Speech transcription runs on your Mac.

**Audience:** Mac users who write throughout the day and want voice input that
fits their existing workflow. Especially useful for developers, writers, and
people who prefer speaking to typing.

**Core line:** Say it once. Keep going.

## Message pillars

- **Dictate in place.** Hold a key, speak, release. Double-tap to keep listening.
- **Private by default.** Audio stays on your Mac for speech transcription.
- **Choose your model.** Use Whisper, Parakeet, or Qwen3-ASR; choose a model
  that fits your Mac.
- **No subscription.** Free and open source under the MIT License.

## Short descriptions

**One line**
Private, local-first voice dictation for Mac.

**Short**
Waveform turns speech into text in the app you are already using. Hold a
shortcut, speak, release. Your voice is transcribed on your Mac.

**Press blurb**
Waveform is a free, open-source dictation app for Apple Silicon Macs. It puts
spoken words into the focused app, runs speech transcription locally, and lets
you choose from Whisper, Parakeet, and Qwen3-ASR models.

## Product facts

- Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or newer.
- Speech model weights download separately; setup requires an internet
  connection. Some optional speech engines need an additional runtime.
- Optional AI Polish can run locally or through OpenRouter. When OpenRouter is
  selected, the text being polished is sent to the chosen hosted model; audio is
  not sent.
- Model downloads and update checks use the network.
- Waveform is pre-1.0; behavior may change between releases.

## Claim boundaries

Say **“speech transcription runs on your Mac”** or **“your voice stays on your
Mac”**. Do not imply every feature works offline: downloads, update checks, and
optional hosted AI Polish use the network. Avoid speed or accuracy guarantees;
results depend on the selected model and hardware. Text insertion works with
standard editable fields; secure fields and apps that block synthetic input may
behave differently.

## Calls to action

- Secondary: **View the source** — GitHub repository.
- Support: **Buy me a coffee** — optional support link from the README.
